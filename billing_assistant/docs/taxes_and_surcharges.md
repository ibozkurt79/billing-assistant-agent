# Taxes and Surcharges (Verizon mobile)

Source: https://www.verizon.com/support/surcharges/
Summarized for interview practice on 2026-10-29. Check verizon.com for current terms.

## Taxes vs surcharges
Government taxes and fees are set by federal, state and local governments; Verizon only bills and collects them.
Surcharges are Verizon's own charges, not taxes or government-imposed fees.
Both can vary by the state and locality of the service address, and both can change over time.

## Regulatory Charge
Helps cover government charges Verizon pays, such as number administration and license fees.
From November 1, 2026 it rises from $0.21 to $0.26 per line per month for voice-capable devices.

## Federal and State Universal Service Charges
Recover charges the government places on Verizon to support universal service programs.

## Administrative and Telco Recovery Charge
Recovers regulatory and industry program costs (such as E911 and number portability), property taxes and network costs.
It is charged per line and Verizon keeps it; it is not a tax.
It was previously called the Administrative Charge and was renamed on June 3, 2022. Verizon reviews and adjusts it periodically.
