# Fees and Payments (Verizon mobile, postpay)

Source: https://www.verizon.com/support/pay-bill-faqs/ 
Summarized for interview practice on 2026-10-29. Check verizon.com for current terms.

## Late payment fee
If payment is not received by the due date, a late fee is charged where state law allows.
The fee is the greater of two amounts: up to 5% of the unpaid balance per month, or a flat $10 per month.

## Getting a late fee removed
A late fee can be disputed on the Bill Details page in My Verizon.
It cannot be removed if the requester is not the Account Owner or Account Manager, has been a customer for less than 2 years, was billed more than 30 days ago, or already received a late-fee credit in the last 6 months.

## Due date
The due date is on the first page of the bill. Account Owners and Account Managers can change it, except during bill processing (the 4 days after a billing period ends).
Customers not on Auto Pay get an email or text reminder 2 days before the due date.

## Payment fees
Paying in My Verizon, in a store, or by dialing #PMT is free.
Paying through Customer Service costs a $10 Agent Assistance fee.
Paying cash through third-party locations (CheckFreePay, Western Union) may cost up to $3.

## Returned payment fee
A returned check costs $20 in CO, CT, ID, IN, NY and UT; $25 in AZ, CA, FL, IL, LA, MI, MO, NH, NV, OK, RI and WV; and $30 in all other states.

## Collections
If an account is sent to collections, a collection fee may apply, capped at the legal maximum and never more than 18%.
