# Auto Pay Discount (Verizon mobile)

Source: https://www.verizon.com/support/auto-pay-faqs/
Summarized for interview practice on 2026-10-29. Check verizon.com for current terms.

## Requirements
The discount requires both Auto Pay and paper-free billing.
Auto Pay can charge a bank account, debit card or credit card, but only a bank account or a Verizon Visa Card qualifies for the discount.
Debit card enrollments made before February 14, 2024 keep the discount.

## Discount amounts
Up to $10 per line per month on myPlan Unlimited Ultimate, Unlimited Ultimate 1.0, Unlimited Plus and Unlimited Welcome.
Up to $5 per line per month on older plans such as 5G Get More, 5G Do More, 5G Play More, 5G Start, Welcome Unlimited and One Unlimited for iPhone.
5G Home and LTE Home internet lines get $10 per line per month.

## When the discount starts
The discount starts with the next bill cycle after enrolling.

## Losing the discount
The discount amount depends on the plan, so changing plans can change it.
Paying a bill once with a credit card online or in the My Verizon app removes the discount for that bill, and it shows up as a charge on the next bill.
