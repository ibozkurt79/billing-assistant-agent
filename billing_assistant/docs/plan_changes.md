# Billing Cycle, First Bill and Plan Changes (Verizon mobile)

Source: https://www.verizon.com/support/view-bill-online-faqs/
Summarized for interview practice on 2026-10-29. Check verizon.com for current terms.

## Billing cycle
The billing cycle starts on the day service is activated and repeats on the same dates every month.
For example, service activated on November 19 has a billing period of November 19 to December 18.

## Plans are billed in advance
The monthly plan charge for the upcoming period appears on the current bill.
Usage-based charges, such as international usage, are billed after they happen.

## Why the first bill is higher
A new customer's first bill includes the partial first billing period plus the next full month in advance.
It may also include a one-time activation fee for each device added.

## Adding a line mid-cycle
A line added in the middle of a cycle is charged a prorated amount for the remaining days of the current cycle, plus the full monthly charge for the next cycle.

## Changing plans mid-cycle
When a plan changes mid-cycle, the bill shows prorated charges for both plans: the old plan up to the change date and the new plan from the change date to the end of the cycle.

## One-time charges
One-time charges include activation fees, upgrade fees, device installment payments and accessories. Taxes, fees and surcharges also apply to them.
Some charges are not final by the bill date and can appear on a later bill.
