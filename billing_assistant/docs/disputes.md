# Disputing Charges (Verizon mobile)

Source: https://www.verizon.com/support/customer-agreement/ 
Summarized for interview practice on 2026-10-29. Check verizon.com for current terms.

## How to dispute a charge
Customers can call Verizon to dispute a charge on their bill.
To keep the right to arbitration or small claims court, the dispute must also be sent in writing to the customer service address on the bill, or through the online Notice of Dispute form.

## Paying while a dispute is open
Postpay customers must keep paying all charges, including disputed ones, until the dispute is resolved, unless the law says otherwise.
Exception: charges related to a lost or stolen device do not have to be paid while they are being investigated.

## Disputing a late fee
Late fees are disputed separately, on the Bill Details page in My Verizon. See the Fees and Payments document for who is eligible for a late-fee credit.
